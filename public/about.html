Hello, I’m Nicolas

A project leader with more than ten years in pharma and clinical research, now coordinating digital transformation — and finally building the technical things I used to only manage.

CyprusProject Coordinator, Technology Office · Mefal

THE SHORT VERSION

I have spent over a decade leading complex projects in places where mistakes are expensive and everything is regulated: clinical trials across Africa, India and Eastern Europe, regional operations for a pharmaceutical company, and global studies at two of the world’s largest clinical research organisations.

Today I work in the Technology Office at Mefal, a pharmaceutical company in Cyprus. My job, most days, is to take something tangled — a system, a process, a room full of vendors who disagree — and turn it into something that works.

The technical side is not new to me, just late. I took Computer Science at A Level, came first in Cyprus that year, and then studied Biological Sciences, followed by a master’s in Nanomedicine. The programming itch never went away, so I am now doing the sensible thing and building in public: automations, small web projects and this site.

I also volunteer with Google Developer Group Cyprus, most recently behind a camera at DevFest Cyprus 2026.

WHAT I’M WORKING ON

  • An ERP migration — led from planning through cutover, against a fixed government funding deadline.
  • A loyalty programme for around 130 pharmacies — requirements and coordination across four point-of-sale vendors and a loyalty platform.
  • A digital transformation roadmap — 14 projects over 18 months, with the reporting framework to keep them honest.
  • Technology evaluation — demand forecasting, warehouse management and robotics, assessed for cost, integration risk and compliance.
