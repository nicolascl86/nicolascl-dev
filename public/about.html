THE SHORT VERSION

I lead projects across operations, digital transformation, AI and automation. My job, most days, is to take something tangled — a process, a system, a room full of people who disagree — and turn it into something that works.

The technical side is not new to me, just late. I took Computer Science at A Level, came first in Cyprus that year, and then studied Biology. Twice. The programming itch never went away, so I am now doing the sensible thing and building in public: automations, small web projects and this site.

I also volunteer with Google Developer Group Cyprus, most recently behind a camera at DevFest Cyprus 2026.
