n8n AI News Workflow

Exploring an automated workflow for gathering and organising AI news.

2026Concept & interactive prototype

OVERVIEW

An automation experiment centred on n8n and AI news. The project explores how connected tools and APIs can turn incoming information into a more manageable reading workflow.

The prototype below makes that idea tangible: four incoming articles become three unique stories and one reviewable digest. It illustrates a possible workflow, rather than a deployed n8n integration or measured project outcome.
