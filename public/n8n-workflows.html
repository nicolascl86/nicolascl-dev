Personal automation

n8n Workflows

Daily reading and everyday expenses, connected through n8n. Three workflows that bring useful information and simple confirmations into my Telegram.

2026n8n · Telegram · Google Sheets

OVERVIEW

I use n8n to connect tools around small, repeatable tasks. Two news briefs deliver articles to my Telegram each day, while an expense tracker turns a message to my bot into a row in Google Sheets.

The common thread is a familiar interface: I receive my reading and record expenses in Telegram, with n8n connecting the steps behind the scenes.

  • Cyprus Brief — five articles daily.
  • AI and Automation Brief — five articles daily.
  • expense-tracker — message, log, confirmation.

01 / CYPRUS BRIEF

Daily news · Telegram delivery

A daily window into Cyprus

Cyprus Brief sends me five articles each day on Telegram. It gives my Cyprus reading a regular place in the day, delivered to an app I already use.

  1. 01 / CadenceDaily briefThe workflow runs daily in n8n.
  2. 02 / DeliveryFive articles to TelegramI receive the brief in my Telegram.

02 / AI AND AUTOMATION BRIEF

Daily news · Telegram delivery

Keeping up with AI and automation

My AI and Automation Brief sends five articles daily to my Telegram. It brings a second area of interest into the same reading routine.

  1. 01 / CadenceDaily briefA separate n8n workflow for AI and automation reading.
  2. 02 / DeliveryFive articles to TelegramA daily set of articles delivered to my Telegram.

03 / EXPENSE-TRACKER

Telegram → n8n → Google Sheets → Telegram

From a quick message to a recorded expense

I text an expense to my Telegram bot. The message goes to n8n, which logs the expense in a Google Sheet and sends me a confirmation on Telegram.

  1. 01 / CaptureText the botI send an expense through Telegram.
  2. 02 / ConnectPass it to n8nThe workflow receives the expense message.
  3. 03 / RecordLog to Google Sheetsn8n adds the expense to my sheet.
  4. 04 / ConfirmReply in TelegramI receive confirmation after the expense is logged.

ONE INTERFACE, THREE ROUTINES

These workflows connect daily information delivery and expense capture through Telegram. The briefs bring reading to me; the expense tracker lets me send a quick message and receive confirmation that it has been recorded.
